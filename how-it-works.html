Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about ordering and platform policies.

How are services and providers vetted?

Every listing is reviewed by BrachioTech platform management to verify portfolio authenticity, execution capability, and communication reliability before being published.

What if I have an extensive enterprise or custom requirement?

You can use our Service Request page to submit your detailed project scope. Platform management will assemble the appropriate technical team and issue a formal proposal within 24 hours.

How do payments and milestones work?

Payment terms are agreed directly with the provider based on milestone deliverables (e.g., initial prototype, staging review, and final production delivery).
